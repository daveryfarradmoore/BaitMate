# Realistic swimming fish

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Each `*-realistic.png` is used by `src/components/SwimmingFish.tsx` with a procedural traveling-wave animation. These are animated 2D images, not 3D models or video.

Prompt template:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: realistic fish cutout for an animated fishing app. One [subject]. Whole fish perfectly side-on, head facing LEFT, tail RIGHT, centered horizontally, body straight and naturally suspended swimming in place. Exquisitely realistic wet scales, translucent fin rays, anatomically plausible proportions, lifelike eye, soft underwater studio lighting. Transparent background with actual alpha, no water scenery, no ground shadow, no text, no border. Wide landscape canvas, entire fish including fins and tail inside canvas with 10 percent empty padding. Single fish only.

Subjects and assets:

- `largemouth-bass-realistic.png`: Largemouth bass, olive green with dark horizontal flank stripe and large mouth.
- `smallmouth-bass-realistic.png`: Smallmouth bass, bronze olive with vertical dark flank bars.
- `trout-realistic.png`: Rainbow trout, silvery with pink lateral band and fine black spots. Represents the catalog's general Trout category.
- `atlantic-salmon-realistic.png`: Atlantic salmon, silver flanks dark back scattered black spots.
- `bluegill-realistic.png`: Bluegill sunfish, deep rounded body blue gill cover and orange breast.
- `catfish-realistic.png`: Channel catfish, smooth slate gray skin, barbels and forked tail. Represents the catalog's general Catfish category.
